# Privacy policy

disclosure-gate only runs in subreddits where it's installed. It reads new posts and comments there, the moderator list, and, if that exemption is on, whether a poster is an approved user.

For each post it asks about, it stores the ID of its own comment and whether OP has replied. These records are deleted after 30 days, or when the app is uninstalled. The moderator list is cached for 15 minutes.

If the confirmed comment uses `{{reply}}`, OP's reply is quoted publicly in the app's comment.

Nothing is sent anywhere outside Reddit, and nothing is shared or sold.

Questions: [open an issue](https://github.com/Watchful1/disclosure-gate/issues) or message u/Watchful1.
