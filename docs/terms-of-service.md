## 1. About this app

`disclosure-gate` is a Reddit moderation app built on the [Devvit](https://developers.reddit.com/) platform. It asks post authors to disclose their use of AI tools before their post is published. It is operated by the GitHub user [Watchful1](https://github.com/Watchful1). The source code is published at <https://github.com/Watchful1/disclosure-gate> under the BSD-3-Clause license.

## 2. What the app does

In subreddits where moderators have installed and enabled it, the app:

- optionally removes new posts, and posts a comment asking the author to reply with a disclosure
- when the author replies, edits that comment (by default quoting the reply) and approves the post, unless another moderator or bot has removed it in the meantime.

Moderators configure the comment text, which exemptions apply, and whether posts are removed and comments pinned.
